During his postdoc in the <a href="https://starklab.org/" style="text-decoration: underline; font-weight: 600;">Stark lab</a> (IMP, Vienna), Vincent specialized in the analysis of Transcription Factor binding motifs, and he will now use this expertise to dissect the cascade of transcriptional events underlying the initiation and long-term maintenance of epigenetically initiated cancers, using Drosophila melanogaster as a model.
