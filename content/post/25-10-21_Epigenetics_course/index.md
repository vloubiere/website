This one-week workshop aims to introduce the various aspects of epigenetic
research via a highly interactive approach that will engage the students in lively
discussions with a set of invited speakers chosen among the world leaders
in the field. Students will go deep in the research topics of the speakers by
combining mentored analysis of their published work with first hand private
discussions with the speakers after an open seminar. While one day will be
devoted to presentation of careers in academia, we will dedicate a full day to
discussing alternative paths in scientific publishing in scientific writing.
This year, we will focus on how the epigenome and nuclear architecture
are regulated in response to DNA damage, mechanical stress and during
developmental processes.

INVITED SPEAKERS:
- Sophie Polo, Epigenetics and Cell fate Unit, Paris - France
- Rebecca Eccles, Cancer Grand Challenges, London - UK
- Angelika Feldmann, DKFZ, Heidelberg - Germany
- Sara Wickström, Max Planck Institute, Münster - Germany
- Bernard de Massy, Institute of Human Genetics, Montpellier - France

Organizers: Charlène Boumendil & Giacomo Cavalli - Institute of Human Genetics - Montpellier, FR.
